One line, not a career change

Analytical responsibility since 2001

Data analysis is not a change of profession for me: I studied economics and business management, and today I apply that knowledge with different tools.

It began with a university degree in economics and business management — assessed by swissuniversities as equivalent to a Swiss bachelor. As deputy head of cash-in-transit at a major Ukrainian bank came route planning by effort and risk, branch metrics and systematic incident analysis; in risk and process management at DIYESA, stock discrepancies, inventory data and ERP analyses across 300 employees.

The questions have stayed the same: where does the deviation arise, which process creates it, what does it cost per year. Only the tools are new — SQL, Tableau and Power BI.
